## B. Hull: Vidal de Negreiros (VLCC) — main hull (decision D3, agreed)

What it is (R2 only; nothing here from memory): a VLCC ("Very Large Crude Carrier"), i.e. a crude-oil tanker
("navio petroleiro"). Its 1:90 model was tested at IPT São Paulo (captive, yaw-rotating and rigid-hawser tests),
the tests being commissioned/funded by Petrobras (R2 p. 14: "contando com financiamento da Petrobras"; p. 136).
Leite et al. note that the MARIN "VLCC-1" is practically identical in shape (R2 p. 61) yet measured C_Y = 0.60 vs 0.84 —
probably a scale effect (R2 p. 61–62). So C_Y carries real uncertainty; Simos finds it hardly affects stability (p. 190).
